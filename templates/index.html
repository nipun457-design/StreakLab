Install on Your Phone for Background Notifications

Web Push notifications can wake your device and alert you at scheduled times without keeping the website open.

iPhone & iPad (iOS 16.4+)
  1. Open StreakLab in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button (box with upward arrow).
  3. Select Add to Home Screen.
  4. Open StreakLab from your Home Screen and tap Enable Mobile Push.
Android (Chrome, Edge, Firefox)
  1. Tap the browser menu (⋮).
  2. Tap Install App or Add to Home screen.
  3. Tap Enable Mobile Push and allow notifications.
How StreakLab Reminders Work

StreakLab supports both in-browser alerts and native mobile Web Push:

  • Tab Activity: Standard in-browser notifications require StreakLab to be open or active in a browser tab.
  • Mobile Web Push: When installed as a mobile app (PWA), notifications are delivered through the push service to your operating system.
  • In-App Alert: If notifications are disabled or unsupported, timely in-app alert banners appear automatically.
  • Duplicate Prevention: Reminders are automatically suppressed once a habit is completed for today.
